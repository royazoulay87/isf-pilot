Colors & Social Choices

The game

When you have the ball, press and hold the player you want to throw to, then release to make the throw. The goal of the group is to make as many successful passes as possible.

Before you begin

After each game, you will make a few choices and answer brief questions about how you feel and about the other players.

Practice the throw

Press and hold the Practice Player; release when the moving border reaches the area you want. In the real games you have 10 seconds per pass.
